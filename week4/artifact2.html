2. Exam-day strategy

Knowledge gets you to the door; strategy gets you through it.

Read the last line first

The final sentence of a long question holds what is actually being asked. Read it first, then read the scenario hunting only for what that line needs.

There is no going back

On Cisco exams you cannot return to a previous question. Commit to each answer before moving on, and never leave one blank: an unanswered question earns nothing.

Eliminate before you choose

Most questions contain two answers that are clearly wrong. Remove them first; the planted trap is usually one of the two survivors. The trap lines in every Test Prep tab of this sequence were training for this moment.

Simulations: verify, do not assume

In hands-on items, configure, then prove it with the right show command before moving on. Treat it exactly like your Packet Tracer labs: the verification checklist is part of the answer.

Budget your clock

Plan roughly a minute per standard question so the simulations get the extra time they need. If a question stalls you, choose your best answer and move: one question is never worth five.

Confirm the details first

Exam length, question count, price, and the current blueprint version can change. Check them on Cisco's official CCNA page before you schedule.
